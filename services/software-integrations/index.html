Can we keep our current tools?+

Often, yes. We investigate the available APIs, export options, permissions and vendor constraints before deciding what can be connected reliably.

What if an API is unavailable?+

We identify that during discovery. An approved import/export workflow may be appropriate, or the integration may need to be excluded or separately scoped.

Who owns the finished system?+

Source access, intellectual property, third-party licenses and handover responsibilities are explicitly agreed in the contract before work begins.
