WHAT WE NEED TO START

A project owner, representative authorized documents, clear access restrictions and a defined reporting workflow.

WHERE THE SCOPE STOPS

This is administrative software, not operational technology. Live grid or equipment control, SCADA/PLC connections, safety decisions and regulated engineering sign-off are outside the example. Export controls, critical-infrastructure restrictions and offshore access conditions are reviewed where applicable.

The timeline is an example, not a delivery guarantee. Integration access, client content and approvals affect the agreed schedule.

Does this connect to live equipment?+

Not in the starting scope. The focus is project and service administration using approved documents or data exports.

Can AI summarize technical documents?+

A bounded assistant can help locate and summarize approved sources with references. Qualified people remain responsible for engineering interpretations and approvals.

Can we begin with a single project?+

Yes. One portfolio, workflow or document class is a useful way to test the approach before considering a broader rollout.
